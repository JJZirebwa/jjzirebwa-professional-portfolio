# Healthcare strategy case studies

## Public-facing summary

My healthcare strategy evidence comes mainly from Health Innovation East, where technology propositions had to be assessed through evidence, market context, stakeholder needs and practical adoption logic.

## Case-study themes

| Theme | Evidence |
|---|---|
| AI/MedTech governance | Authored internal AI Toolkit guidance-support work covering evidence, regulatory framing, information governance, interoperability and risk. |
| Cardiovascular adoption logic | Independently drafted QOF strategy material and drafted or supported PulseOn AF-monitoring strategy work. |
| Business-case support | Co-authored Charco/CUE1 evidence, pathway and value-framing material with senior colleagues. |
| Market intelligence | CB Insights and secondary-research work using company searches, structured company lists, reusable research views and trend summaries. |
| Competitor analysis | Independently authored internal competitor analyses comparing products across validation, workflow fit, maturity and adoption factors. |

## What ties the work together

Across these examples, I was usually trying to make a broad health-innovation question easier to assess:

- what problem the product or pathway is addressing
- what evidence supports the claim
- who would need to trust or use the intervention
- where it might fit in a care pathway
- what competing options already exist
- what governance or adoption risks remain
- what evidence would be needed next
