# Dissertation extract policy

## Current handling

This portfolio uses high-level summary pages that describe the research question, methods, limitations and findings without reproducing restricted material.

## Why extracts are withheld

The dissertation included genomics-linked cohort context, small counts (patient-identifiable) and restricted-environment study details. For public release, I have withheld sensitive granularity to respect Genomics England data agreements and protect patient data.

## What is excluded

- restricted exported data
- participant-level data
- cohort counts and small subgroup counts
- model result tables derived from restricted cohorts
- raw dissertation figures unless manually cleared
- raw ICD/OPCS tables
