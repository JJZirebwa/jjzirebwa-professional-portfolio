# Governance and limitations

## Summary

The final-year project was research-only. It was not a clinical diagnostic tool, treatment-selection aid or validated AI system.

## Governance principles

- The work remained inside approved secure research arrangements.
- Outputs were treated as academic research evidence.
- Target definitions were documented as research constructs.
- Model interpretation was bounded by rare-disease sparsity, imbalance and routine-data limitations.

## Limitations

| Limitation | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Routine hospital coding is incomplete | Hospital codes reflect care and administration, not the full clinical phenotype. |
| Rare-disease cohort constraints | Small and imbalanced samples make performance estimates unstable. |
| No external validation | Results cannot be generalised as a validated model. |
| No diagnostic replacement | ECG, clinical history, family history and specialist interpretation remain central. |
| Sensitive data context | Public material must avoid genomics-linked tables, counts and exported data. |
