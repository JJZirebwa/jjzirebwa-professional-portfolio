# Research Internship Overview

## Summary

I supported computational toxicology data preparation during a six-week research internship connected to the DioScor platform by ConsoneAI.

## Context

The internship was based in preclinical and translational research, where animal, human and chemical toxicity evidence needed to be organised into reviewable forms.

## My Contribution

Contribution areas include:

- mapping public or external toxicology data fields to high-level information needs without exposing proprietary schema
- reviewing dose-toxicity data sources
- extracting and organising data into Excel/CSV-style outputs
- supporting data cleaning and consistency checks
- using lightweight Python-supported workflows where applicable
- communicating findings through structured outputs and presentation material

## Methods / Approach

The work required translating inconsistent external data into a more usable structure:

- identify relevant source fields
- compare terminology and grouping differences
- map fields to high-level categories without exposing proprietary schema
- clean or split datasets where needed
- document limitations and missingness
- prepare analysis-ready summaries

## Outputs

- internal mapping notes
- organised non-public data outputs
- dose-toxicity summaries
- presentation-style communication material, not included here

## Skills Demonstrated

- data extraction
- structured data mapping
- Excel/CSV organisation
- lightweight scripting literacy
- computational toxicology awareness
- scientific communication

## What This Shows

This internship shows my early move from biomedical science into data-enabled research and structured analytical work.
