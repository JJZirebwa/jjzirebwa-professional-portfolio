# Competitor Analysis and Horizon Scanning

## Summary

I independently developed internal competitor-analysis materials and supported horizon-scanning work across health technologies, comparing products by purpose, evidence, functionality, pathway fit, regulatory position, integration considerations and commercial maturity.

## Context

Beyond being a feature comparison, competitor analyses need to consider clinical need, evidence quality, stakeholder trust, workflow integration, regulatory readiness and whether the product can realistically fit a service pathway.

## My Contribution

I authored internal competitor-analysis materials across digital health, diagnostics, AI-enabled tools and MedTech, including major competitor-analysis work during the placement.

## Methods / Approach

Common comparison dimensions:

- clinical or operational problem addressed
- target user and pathway position
- function and product scope
- evidence and clinical validation signals
- interoperability and integration needs
- regulatory position where public
- market maturity and company signals
- pricing or value logic where safe and available
- adoption risks and open questions
