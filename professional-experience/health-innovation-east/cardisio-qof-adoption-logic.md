# Cardisio / QOF Adoption Logic

## Public-Facing Summary

This case study summarises my work around AI-ECG competitor analysis and Quality and Outcomes Framework adoption logic.

## Context

AI-enabled cardiac diagnostics need to be assessed through clinical evidence, workflow fit, stakeholder confidence, regulatory position and practical adoption routes. The QOF context added a primary-care incentive and cardiovascular prevention lens.

## My Contribution

I independently developed internal competitor-analysis material and drafted internal strategy proposal material exploring how 2025/26 QOF changes could shape adoption logic for a cardiovascular diagnostic innovation.

## Methods / Approach

The method included:

- comparing functionality and intended use
- reviewing public evidence and performance claims
- considering human-in-the-loop workflow implications
- mapping stakeholder decision drivers
- translating QOF changes into practical adoption questions
- considering stakeholder perspectives where public or internally approved

## Outputs

- competitor-analysis material
- QOF explainer and strategy framing
- adoption/value logic
- stakeholder decision-driver summary

## Skills Demonstrated

- cardiovascular innovation analysis
- evidence comparison
- policy-to-adoption translation
- competitor landscape reasoning
- structured commercial writing

## What This Shows

This case shows that I can connect evidence, policy and market positioning without reducing adoption to simple performance claims.
