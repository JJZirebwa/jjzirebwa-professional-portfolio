# Charco / CUE1 Business-Case Support

## Summary

This case study summarises my co-authored and support-based work around CUE1, a Parkinson's cueing device, with emphasis on evidence synthesis, patient independence, quality of life, pathway fit and adoption logic.

## Context

Parkinson's disease can affect mobility, falls risk, independence and quality of life. For an innovation to be considered by healthcare stakeholders, evidence, pathway fit, patient benefit and value assumptions need to be critically assessed.

## My Contribution

I supported strategy and business-case materials alongside senior colleagues, connecting clinical evidence, patient pathway needs, national or local Integrated Care Board (ICB) priority logic, falls/admissions burden and value framing into a structured narrative.

## Methods / Approach

The approach included:

- summarising the Parkinson's/falls context
- reviewing evidence around patient independence and quality of life
- considering where the device might fit in a pathway
- supporting value framing and business-case structure
- identifying evidence-generation and adoption questions
- keeping assumptions distinct from validated outcomes
- evaluating needs in different ICB patches and framing alignment

## Outputs

- evidence summary support
- pathway and value framing
- business-case narrative support
- regional facing alignment piece
- adoption-readiness questions

## Skills Demonstrated

- evidence synthesis
- business-case support
- patient-centred value framing
- adoption logic
- clear written communication

## What This Shows

This case shows that I can help turn clinical and product evidence into a structured adoption argument while respecting the limits of available data.
