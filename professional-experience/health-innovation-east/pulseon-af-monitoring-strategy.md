# PulseOn AF Monitoring Strategy

## Public-Facing Summary

This case study summarises drafted and supported strategy material around atrial fibrillation monitoring, post-stroke risk, pathway gaps, competitor limitations and potential adoption/value logic.

## Context

AF detection and monitoring are crucial to cardiovascular prevention, wearable or remote monitoring technologies, service pathway design and evidence confidence. Adoption depends on whether a technology can address a real pathway gap in addition to any data it produces.

## My Contribution

I drafted and supported strategy material that framed the unmet need, evidence context, competitor limitations and potential value logic for adoption conversations.

## Methods / Approach

The reconstructed approach included:

- defining the unmet need and pathway gap
- reviewing competitor and alternative-monitoring options
- identifying limitations in existing pathways or products
- linking evidence to stakeholder decision questions
- distinguishing assumptions from validated cost or outcome claims
- framing pilot logic and evidence-generation needs
