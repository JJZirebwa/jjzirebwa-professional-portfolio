# AI Toolkit

## Public-Facing Summary

I authored a practical internal AI/MedTech guidance resource during a Health Innovation East commercial placement. The work translated regulatory, evidence, data-governance and implementation considerations into usable support material for innovation advisory work.

## Context

AI-enabled medical technologies create a difficult translation problem. Innovators need to understand the technical product, but also regulation, evidence standards, clinical safety, information governance, interoperability and monitoring after deployment.

## My Contribution

I drafted and authored practical AI/MedTech guidance-support material for internal innovation advisory use. The work was not formal regulatory advice, legal advice or a substitute for specialist clinical-safety review.

## Methods / Approach

Evidence to support the formulation of the document was compiled from a number of official publications by regulatory bodies in addition to the East of England AI Forum which I attended throughout my placement.

The toolkit covered topics including:

- AI as a medical device and software as a medical device concepts
- MHRA and UKCA regulatory framing
- NICE evidence expectations
- DTAC considerations
- DCB0129 and DCB0160 clinical safety standards
- information governance and data protection considerations
- interoperability and integration
- risk management
- post-market monitoring
- stakeholder engagement and value demonstration

## Outputs

- structured guidance material
- topic summaries
- practical checklists and explanatory sections
- governance-aware framing for innovator support

## Skills Demonstrated

- AI-health governance literacy
- regulated-health writing
- evidence standards interpretation
- ability to translate technical and policy material into practical guidance
- claim-boundary discipline

## What This Shows

This work demonstrates my ability to make AI/MedTech topics understandable while keeping clear boundaries around regulatory, legal and clinical-safety authority.
